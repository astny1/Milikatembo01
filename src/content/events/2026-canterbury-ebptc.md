Milika will present doctoral research on bibliotherapy in Zambia, where the practice often appears without formal frameworks, training models or institutional recognition. The paper describes this condition as *bibliotherapy without construction* and interprets the lived experience of librarians and mental-health therapists in a Zambian university setting through hermeneutic phenomenology.

She also serves as session chair for *Hope Beyond Grief* during the conference programme.
